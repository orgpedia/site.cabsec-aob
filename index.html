About the data
  • Subjects are the numbered entries of the Second Schedule of the Government of India (Allocation of Business) Rules, 1961, counted after each of its 386 amendments.
  • Colour is the ministry's sector:
  • Before 1961 the ministries come from the cabinet lists of 1947–1957; their sizes are worked back from 1961, so they are approximate.
  • Before 2004 the readings of the scanned gazettes still have known gaps.
  • Prime Ministers' terms: a change belongs to the term in which it was notified.
  • In office: pmindia.gov.in, presidentofindia.gov.in, cabsec.gov.in.
