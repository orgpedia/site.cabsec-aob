What it did to the ministries

  • bornMinistry of Non Resident Indian Affairs is created (1 subject) change 7, 18 · histories: NRI Affairs
  • bornMinistry of Panchayati Raj is created (1 subject) change 7, 18 · histories: Panchayati Raj
  • spun offMinistry of Company Affairs is separated from Ministry of Finance (11 subjects) change 1, 5, 11, 14, 16 · histories: Finance, Corporate Affairs
  • spun offMinistry of Tourism is separated from Ministry of Tourism and Culture (3 subjects) change 2, 8, 12, 20 · histories: Culture, Tourism
  • spun offMinistry of Urban Employment and Poverty Alleviation is separated from Ministry of Urban Development and Poverty Alleviation (5 subjects) change 9, 21 · histories: Housing and Urban Affairs, Housing & Urban Poverty
  • absorbedThe Ministry of Disinvestment is folded into Finance. change 4, 5, 12, 14, 16 · histories: Disinvestment, Finance
  • renamedMinistry of Tourism and Culture is renamed Ministry of Culture change 2, 8, 12 · histories: Culture
  • renamedDepartment of Development of North Eastern Region is renamed Ministry of Development of North Eastern Region change 3, 10, 13 · histories: North East
  • renamedMinistry of Labour is renamed Ministry of Labour and Employment change 6, 17 · histories: Labour and Employment
  • renamedMinistry of Urban Development and Poverty Alleviation is renamed Ministry of Urban Development change 9, 21 · histories: Housing and Urban Affairs
