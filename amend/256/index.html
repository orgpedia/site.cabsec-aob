What it did to the ministries

  • bornA Department of Development of North Eastern Region is created. change 10, 19, 23 · histories: North East
  • splitMinistry of Small Scale Industries and Agro and Rural Industries splits into Ministry of Agro and Rural Industries (6) and Ministry of Small Scale Industries (3) change 2, 8, 12, 19, 23 · histories: Small Scale Industries and Agro and Rural Industries, Agro and Rural Industries, Small Scale Industries
  • spun offMinistry of Food Processing Industries is separated from Ministry of Agriculture (8 subjects) change 1, 2, 6, 11, 12, 13 · histories: Agriculture, Food Processing
  • spun offMinistry of Tourism is separated from Ministry of Tourism and Culture (3 subjects) change 4, 9, 19, 20, 23 · histories: Culture, Tourism
  • mergedMinistry of Coal and Ministry of Mines merge into Ministry of Coal and Mines change 3, 7, 14 · histories: Coal, Mines, Coal and Mines
  • renamedMinistry of Tourism and Culture is renamed Ministry of Culture change 4, 9, 19, 23 · histories: Culture
  • renamedDepartment of Disinvestment is renamed Ministry of Disinvestment change 5, 10, 19, 23 · histories: Disinvestment
