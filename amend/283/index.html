Order dated 16 February 2006 · S.O. 219(E), published 16 February 2006 · in force from 16 February 2006

19 changes: 8 to the First Schedule, 11 to the Second Schedule. 19 applied

What it did to the ministries

  • spun offMinority Affairs is carved out of Social Justice. change 3, 12, 15, 16 · histories: Social Justice, Minority Affairs
  • spun offWomen and Child Development becomes a ministry of its own, out of HRD. change 2, 7, 11, 18 · histories: Education, Women & Child
  • absorbedMinistry of Tourism joins Ministry of Tourism and Culture (52 → 55) change 1, 6, 9, 17 · histories: Tourism, Culture
  • renamedDepartment of Ocean Development is renamed Ministry of Ocean Development change 4, 5, 8, 13, 14, 18 · histories: Earth Sciences
  • renamedMinistry of Culture is renamed Ministry of Tourism and Culture change 1, 6, 9, 17 · histories: Culture
