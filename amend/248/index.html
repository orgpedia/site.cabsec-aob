14 changes: 7 to the First Schedule, 7 to the Second Schedule. 14 applied

What it did to the ministries

  • splitMinistry of Culture, Youth Affairs and Sports splits into Ministry of Tourism and Culture (50) and Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports (25) change 2, 4, 7, 11, 14 · histories: Culture, Youth Affairs and Sports, Culture, Skills, Youth & Sports
  • splitMinistry of Mines and Minerals splits into Ministry of Coal (13) and Ministry of Mines (7) change 1, 3, 8, 10 · histories: Mines and Minerals, Coal, Mines
  • absorbedMinistry of Urban Employment and Poverty Alleviation joins Ministry of Urban Development and Poverty Alleviation (33 → 40) change 5, 6, 12 · histories: Urban Poverty, Housing and Urban Affairs
  • absorbedMinistry of Tourism joins Ministry of Tourism and Culture (3 subjects) change 4, 11 · histories: Tourism, Culture
  • renamedMinistry of Urban Development is renamed Ministry of Urban Development and Poverty Alleviation change 5, 12 · histories: Housing and Urban Affairs
