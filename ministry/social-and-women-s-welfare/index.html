Ministry of Social and Women's Welfare

Ministry · Social & culture · 1979–1985 · its line since 1947 · abolished

Earlier: Ministry of Social Welfare 1979–1985

Before 2004 the readings of the scanned gazettes have known gaps, so some changes may be missing or read loosely.

Its line: Education and Youth Services 1947–1971 → Education and Social Welfare 1971–1979 → Social and Women's Welfare 1979–1985

The units it carries on, through its splits and mergers, back to where it began. Its changes and its story in cells below follow the whole line.

Its line goes on in: Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment since 1985

Came from: Ministry of Scientific Research, Ministry of Scientific Research & Cultural Affairs, Ministry of Education and Social Welfare

Gave subjects to: Ministry of Scientific Research & Cultural Affairs, Department of Social Security, Department of Culture, Department of Science and Technology, Ministry of Education and Culture

Succeeded by: Ministry of Welfare, Ministry of Human Resource Development

Where a merger or a split kept one unit going, it is the one that brought or kept the larger share of subjects.
