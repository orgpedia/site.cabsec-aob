Ministry of Surface Transport

Ministry · Infrastructure & energy · 1986–2004 · its line since 1947 · abolished

On the chartIts section of the RulesAll ministries

Before 2004 the readings of the scanned gazettes have known gaps, so some changes may be missing or read loosely.

Its line: Transport 1947–1966 → Transport and Aviation 1966–1967 → Shipping and Transport 1967–1985 → Transport 1985–1986 → Surface Transport 1986–2004

The units it carries on, through its splits and mergers, back to where it began. Its changes and its story in cells below follow the whole line.

Came from: Ministry of Communications, Ministry of Civil Aviation, Ministry of Tourism & Civil Aviation, Ministry of Railways, Ministry of Transport

Gave subjects to: Ministry of Railways, Department of Posts and Telegraphs, Ministry of Civil Aviation, Ministry of Tourism & Civil Aviation, Ministry of Civil Aviation, Ministry of Railways, Ministry of Shipping, Ministry of Road Transport and Highways

Where a merger or a split kept one unit going, it is the one that brought or kept the larger share of subjects.
