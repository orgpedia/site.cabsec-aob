Ministry of Cultural Affairs

Ministry · Social & culture · 1985 · its line since 1947 · abolished

Earlier: Department of Culture 1985

On the chartIts section of the RulesAll ministries

Before 2004 the readings of the scanned gazettes have known gaps, so some changes may be missing or read loosely.

Its line: Education and Youth Services 1947–1971 → Culture 1971–1979 → Education and Culture 1979–1985 → Cultural Affairs 1985

The units it carries on, through its splits and mergers, back to where it began. Its changes and its story in cells below follow the whole line.

Came from: Ministry of Scientific Research, Ministry of Scientific Research & Cultural Affairs, Ministry of Education and Social Welfare, Ministry of Education and Culture

Gave subjects to: Ministry of Scientific Research & Cultural Affairs, Department of Social Security, Ministry of Education and Social Welfare, Department of Science and Technology, Ministry of Education

Succeeded by: Ministry of Human Resource Development

Where a merger or a split kept one unit going, it is the one that brought or kept the larger share of subjects.
