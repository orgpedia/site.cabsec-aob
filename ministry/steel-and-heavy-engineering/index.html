Ministry of Steel and Heavy Engineering

Ministry · Economy & industry · 1969–1971 · its line since 1963 · abolished

On the chartIts section of the RulesAll ministries

Before 2004 the readings of the scanned gazettes have known gaps, so some changes may be missing or read loosely.

Its line: Steel and Mines 1963–1966 → Iron and Steel 1966–1967 → Steel, Mines & Metals 1967–1969 → Steel and Heavy Engineering 1969–1971

The units it carries on, through its splits and mergers, back to where it began. Its changes and its story in cells below follow the whole line.

Its line goes on in: Ministry of Steel 1991–1998, Ministry of Steel since 1999

Came from: Ministry of Steel & Heavy Industries, Ministry of Mines and Metals, Ministry of Steel, Mines & Metals

Gave subjects to: Ministry of Mines and Metals, Ministry of Petroleum and Chemicals and Mines & Metals

Succeeded by: Ministry of Steel and Mines

Where a merger or a split kept one unit going, it is the one that brought or kept the larger share of subjects.
