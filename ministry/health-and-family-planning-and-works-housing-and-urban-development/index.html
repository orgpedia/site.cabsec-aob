Ministry of Health and Family Planning, and Works Housing & Urban Development

Ministry · Social & culture · 1947–1971 · abolished

Earlier: Ministry of Health, Family Planning and Urban Development 1968–1969 · Ministry of Health and Family Planning 1966–1968 · Ministry of Health 1961–1966

On the chartIts section of the RulesAll ministries

Before 2004 the readings of the scanned gazettes have known gaps, so some changes may be missing or read loosely.

Its line goes on in: Ministry of Health and Family Welfare since 1971, Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs since 1971

Came from: Ministry of Works, Housing & Supply

Succeeded by: Ministry of Health and Family Planning, Ministry of Works and Housing
