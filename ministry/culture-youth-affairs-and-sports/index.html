Ministry of Culture, Youth Affairs and Sports

Ministry · Social & culture · 1999–2000 · abolished

On the chartIts section of the RulesAll ministries

Before 2004 the readings of the scanned gazettes have known gaps, so some changes may be missing or read loosely.

Its line goes on in: Ministry of Culture since 2000, Ministry of Skill Development, Entrepreneurship, Youth Affairs and Sports 2000–2014, Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship since 2014, Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports since 2014

Came from: Ministry of Human Resource Development

Succeeded by: Ministry of Tourism and Culture, Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports

Where a merger or a split kept one unit going, it is the one that brought or kept the larger share of subjects.
