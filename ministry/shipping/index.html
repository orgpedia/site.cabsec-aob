Ministry · Infrastructure & energy · 2000–2004 · abolished

Its line goes on in: Ministry of Ports, Shipping and Waterways since 2009

Came from: Ministry of Surface Transport

Succeeded by: Ministry of Shipping, Road Transport and Highways

Where a merger or a split kept one unit going, it is the one that brought or kept the larger share of subjects.
