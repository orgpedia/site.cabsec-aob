Ministry of Works, Mines and Power

Ministry · Infrastructure & energy · 1947–1952 · abolished

On the chartAll ministries

Before 2004 the readings of the scanned gazettes have known gaps, so some changes may be missing or read loosely.

Its line goes on in: Ministry of Steel, Mines & Fuel 1952–1962, Ministry of Works, Housing and Rehabilitation 1952–1964, Ministry of Planning and Programme Implementation 1952–1999, Ministry of Mines & Fuel 1962–1963, Ministry of Steel & Heavy Industries 1962–1963, Ministry of Rehabilitation 1964–1966, Department of Supply 1964–1973, Department of Supply 1973–1985, Ministry of Planning since 1999, Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation since 1999

Succeeded by: Ministry of Works, Housing & Supply, Ministry of Production, Ministry of Planning and River Valley Schemes
