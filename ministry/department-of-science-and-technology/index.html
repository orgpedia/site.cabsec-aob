Its line: Education and Youth Services 1947–1971 → Science and Technology 1971–1975

The units it carries on, through its splits and mergers, back to where it began. Its changes and its story in cells below follow the whole line.

Came from: Ministry of Scientific Research, Ministry of Scientific Research & Cultural Affairs, Ministry of Education

Gave subjects to: Ministry of Scientific Research & Cultural Affairs, Department of Social Security, Ministry of Education and Social Welfare, Department of Culture

Succeeded by: Ministry of Planning

Where a merger or a split kept one unit going, it is the one that brought or kept the larger share of subjects.
