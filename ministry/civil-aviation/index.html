Ministry of Civil Aviation

Ministry · Infrastructure & energy · since 1989 · its line since 1964

Formerly: Ministry of Civil Aviation and Tourism 1991–1998 · Ministry of Civil Aviation 1989–1991

On the chartIts section of the RulesAll ministries

Before 2004 the readings of the scanned gazettes have known gaps, so some changes may be missing or read loosely.

Its line: Civil Aviation 1964–1966 → Transport and Aviation 1966–1967 → Tourism and Civil Aviation 1967–1985 → Transport 1985–1986 → Civil Aviation and Tourism 1986–1989 → Civil Aviation since 1989

The units it carries on, through its splits and mergers, back to where it began. Its changes and its story in cells below follow the whole line.

Came from: Ministry of Transport, Ministry of Tourism, Ministry of Transport & Shipping, Ministry of Railways, Ministry of Civil Aviation

Gave subjects to: Ministry of Transport & Shipping, Ministry of Tourism, Ministry of Surface Transport, Ministry of Railways, Ministry of Tourism

Where a merger or a split kept one unit going, it is the one that brought or kept the larger share of subjects.
