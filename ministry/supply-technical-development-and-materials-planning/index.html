Ministry of Supply, Technical Development & Materials Planning

Ministry · Economy & industry · 1966–1967 · abolished

Earlier: Ministry of Supply and Technical Development 1966

On the chartIts section of the RulesAll ministries

Before 2004 the readings of the scanned gazettes have known gaps, so some changes may be missing or read loosely.

Came from: Ministry of Industry

Succeeded by: Ministry of Industrial Development & Company Affairs, Ministry of Works, Housing & Supply

Where a merger or a split kept one unit going, it is the one that brought or kept the larger share of subjects.

Its changes

  1. 25 January 1966
    Indira Gandhi
    Spun offMinistry of Supply and Technical Development is separated from Ministry of Industry and Supply (5 subjects)
    With: Ministry of Industry and Supply
    Amendment #46 · change 14, 15, 22 · the Rules after it
    The subjects it took (5)
    • Purchase, inspection and shipment of stores for the Central Government other than the items the purchase, Inspection and shipment of which are delegated to other authorities by a general or special order
    • Disposal of surplus stores
    • Residual work of supply and disposal relating to the late war organisations including the Directorate General, Aircraft, including Civil Maintenance Units and Directorate General, Ship Repairs.
    • Technical Development, including Directorate General of Technical Development.
    • Administration of: (a) Directorate General of Supplies and Disposals (b) Government Test House, Alipore, Calcutta (c) India Store Department, the United Kingdom (d) India Supply Mission, the U.S.A.
  2. 25 March 1966
    Indira Gandhi
    RenamedMinistry of Supply and Technical Development is renamed Ministry of Supply, Technical Development & Materials Planning
    Amendment #49 · change 1, 2 · the Rules after it
  3. 25 March 1966
    Indira Gandhi
    GrewMinistry of Supply, Technical Development & Materials Planning grows from 5 to 10 subjects
    Amendment #49 · change 1, 2 · the Rules after it
  4. 14 March 1967
    Indira Gandhi
    SplitMinistry of Supply, Technical Development & Materials Planning splits into Ministry of Industrial Development & Company Affairs (6) and Ministry of Works, Housing & Supply (4)
    With: Ministry of Industrial Development & Company Affairs, Ministry of Works, Housing & Supply
    Amendment #58 · change 2, 6, 8, 9 · the Rules before it
    The subjects that left (10)
    • Technical Development, including Directorate General of Technical Development.
    • Coordinated assessment of demands for raw materials, by sectors. Industries and large units, in relation to particular groups of products and to available capacities.
    • Assessment of domestic availability of raw materials with due regard to the feasibility of import substitution.
    • Assessment of requirements of imports of raw materials, with due allowance for inventories.
    • Determination of principles, priorities and procedures for allocation of raw materials.
    • Any other matters connected with materials planning.
    • Purchase, inspection and shipment of stores for the Central Government other than the items the purchase, inspection and shipment of which are delegated to other authorities by a general or special order.
    • Disposal of surplus stores.
    • Residual work of supply and disposal relating to the late war organisations including the Directorate General, Aircraft, including Civil Maintenance Units and Directorate General, Ship Repairs.
    • Administration of (a) Directorate General of Supplies and Disposals (b) National Test House, Alipore, Calcutta (c) India Supply Mission, the United Kingdom (d) India Supply Mission, the U. S. A.
