Ministry of Commerce & Industry

Ministry · Economy & industry · 1952–1963 · its line since 1947 · abolished

Earlier: Ministry of Commerce & Industry 1952–1961

Its line: Industry & Supply 1947–1952 → Commerce & Industry 1952–1963

The units it carries on, through its splits and mergers, back to where it began. Its changes and its story in cells below follow the whole line.

Its line goes on in: Ministry of Industry 1963–1999, Ministry of Foreign Trade 1963–1973, Ministry of Commerce and Industry since 1999, Ministry of Heavy Industries since 1999, Ministry of Small Scale Industries and Agro and Rural Industries 1999–2001, Ministry of Agro and Rural Industries 2001–2007, Ministry of Small Scale Industries 2001–2007

Came from: Ministry of Commerce

Succeeded by: Ministry of International Trade, Ministry of Industry
