Ministry of Jal Shakti

Ministry · Land, water & rural · since 1985 · its line since 1957

Formerly: Ministry of Water Resources, River Development and Ganga Rejuvenation 2014–2019 · Ministry of Water Resources 1985–2014

Before 2004 the readings of the scanned gazettes have known gaps, so some changes may be missing or read loosely.

Its line: Irrigation & Power 1957–1974 → Energy 1974–1985 → Irrigation and Power 1985 → Jal Shakti since 1985

The units it carries on, through its splits and mergers, back to where it began. Its changes and its story in cells below follow the whole line.

Came from: Ministry of Planning and River Valley Schemes, Ministry of Steel and Mines, Ministry of Petroleum, Chemicals and Fertilizers, Ministry of Irrigation, Ministry of Drinking Water and Sanitation, Ministry of Irrigation and Power

Gave subjects to: Ministry of Agriculture and Irrigation, Ministry of Steel, Mines and Coal, Ministry of Petroleum, Ministry of Steel, Mines and Coal, Ministry of Energy

Where a merger or a split kept one unit going, it is the one that brought or kept the larger share of subjects.
