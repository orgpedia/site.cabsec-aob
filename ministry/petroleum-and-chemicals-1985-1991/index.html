Ministry of Petroleum and Chemicals

Ministry · Infrastructure & energy · 1985–1991 · its line since 1957 · abolished

Earlier: Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas 1985–1989 · Ministry of Petroleum 1985

Before 2004 the readings of the scanned gazettes have known gaps, so some changes may be missing or read loosely.

Its line: Irrigation & Power 1957–1974 → Energy 1974–1985 → Petroleum and Chemicals 1985–1991

The units it carries on, through its splits and mergers, back to where it began. Its changes and its story in cells below follow the whole line.

Its line goes on in: Ministry of Chemicals & Fertilizers since 1991, Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas since 1991

Came from: Ministry of Planning and River Valley Schemes, Ministry of Steel and Mines, Ministry of Petroleum, Chemicals and Fertilizers, Ministry of Energy

Gave subjects to: Ministry of Agriculture and Irrigation, Ministry of Steel, Mines and Coal, Ministry of Steel, Mines and Coal, Ministry of Irrigation and Power

Succeeded by: Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas, Ministry of Chemicals & Fertilizers

Where a merger or a split kept one unit going, it is the one that brought or kept the larger share of subjects.
