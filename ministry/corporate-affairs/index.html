Its subjects

Its subjects today · 19
  1. Administration of the Companies Act, 1956 (1 of 1956).
  2. Administration of the Companies (Donations of National Funds) Act, 1951 (54 of 1951).
  3. Profession of Accountancy [The Chartered Accountants Act, 1949 (38 of 1949)]; Profession of Costs and Works Accountancy [The Cost and Works Accountants Act, 1959(23 of 1959)]; Profession of Company Secretaries [The Company Secretaries Act, 1980 (56 of 1980)].
  4. Collection of Statistics relating to Companies.
  5. Legislation relating to law of Partnership and the exercise of certain functions under Chapter VII of the Indian Partnership Act, 1932 (9 of 1932) in centrally administrated areas. (The administration of the Act vests in the State Governments).
  6. The responsibility of the Centre relating to matters concerning centrally administered areas in respect of any of the above items.
  7. Legislation in relation to societies registration and exercise of functions under the Societies Registration Act, 1860 (21 of 1860) in centrally administered areas.
  8. Competition Commission of India.
  9. The Competition Act, 2002 (12 of 2003).
  10. Serious Frauds Investigation Office.
  11. Administration of Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code.
  12. Administration of Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India.
  13. Administration of National Company Law Appellate Tribunal.
  14. Administration of National Company Law Tribunal.
  15. Administration of Investor Education and Protection Fund Authority.
  16. Indian Institute of Corporate Affairs.
  17. National Financial Reporting Authority.
  18. Administration of the Limited Liability Partnership Act, 2008 (6 of 2009).
  19. Administration of the Companies Act, 2013 (18 of 2013).
