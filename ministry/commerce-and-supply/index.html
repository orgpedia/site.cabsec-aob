Ministry of Commerce and Supply

Ministry · Economy & industry · 1980–1985 · its line since 1973 · abolished

Earlier: Ministry of Commerce 1980–1985

On the chartIts section of the RulesAll ministries

Before 2004 the readings of the scanned gazettes have known gaps, so some changes may be missing or read loosely.

Its line: Commerce 1973–1978 → Commerce and Civil Supplies 1978–1980 → Commerce and Supply 1980–1985

The units it carries on, through its splits and mergers, back to where it began. Its changes and its story in cells below follow the whole line.

Its line goes on in: Ministry of Commerce 1985–1999, Ministry of Supply and Textiles 1985

Came from: Ministry of Foreign Trade, Ministry of Industrial Development, Ministry of Civil Supplies and Cooperation, Ministry of Supply and Rehabilitation, Ministry of Commerce, Civil Supplies and Cooperation

Gave subjects to: Ministry of Industry and Civil Supplies, Ministry of Civil Supplies

Succeeded by: Ministry of Commerce, Ministry of Supply and Textiles

Where a merger or a split kept one unit going, it is the one that brought or kept the larger share of subjects.
