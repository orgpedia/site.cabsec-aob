Ministry of Commerce

Ministry · Economy & industry · 1985–1999 · its line since 1973 · abolished

On the chartIts section of the RulesAll ministries

Before 2004 the readings of the scanned gazettes have known gaps, so some changes may be missing or read loosely.

Its line: Commerce 1973–1978 → Commerce and Civil Supplies 1978–1980 → Commerce and Supply 1980–1985 → Commerce 1985–1999

The units it carries on, through its splits and mergers, back to where it began. Its changes and its story in cells below follow the whole line.

Came from: Ministry of Foreign Trade, Ministry of Industrial Development, Ministry of Civil Supplies and Cooperation, Ministry of Supply and Rehabilitation, Ministry of Supply and Textiles, Ministry of Commerce

Gave subjects to: Ministry of Industry and Civil Supplies, Ministry of Civil Supplies, Ministry of Supply and Textiles, Ministry of Textiles

Succeeded by: Ministry of Commerce and Industry

Where a merger or a split kept one unit going, it is the one that brought or kept the larger share of subjects.

Its story in cells

Only its own changes play, with a caption; the rest of the government changes quietly in the background. The units it dealt with stand out. Click a cell for its subjects in the Rules.
