Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change

Ministry · Land, water & rural · since 1980

Formerly: Ministry of Environment and Forests 1985–2014 · Department of Environment 1982–1985 · Department of Ecology 1982 · Department of Environment 1980–1982

On the chartIts section of the RulesAll ministries

Before 2004 the readings of the scanned gazettes have known gaps, so some changes may be missing or read loosely.

Came from: Department of Science and Technology

Where a merger or a split kept one unit going, it is the one that brought or kept the larger share of subjects.

Its story in cells

Only its own changes play, with a caption; the rest of the government changes quietly in the background. The units it dealt with stand out. Click a cell for its subjects in the Rules.

Its subjects

Its subjects today · 43
  1. Environment and Ecology, including environment in coastal waters, in mangroves and coral reefs but excluding marine environment on the high seas.
  2. Environment Research and Development, education, training, information and awareness.
  3. Environmental Health.
  4. Environmental Impact Assessment.
  5. Forest Development Agency and Joint Forest Management Programme for conservation, management and afforestation.
  6. Survey and Exploration of Natural Resources particularly of Forest, Flora, Fauna, Ecosystems etc.
  7. Bio-diversity Conservation including that of lakes and Wetlands.
  8. Wildlife conservation, preservation, protection planning, research, education, training and awareness including Project Tiger and Project Elephant.
  9. International co-operation on Issues concerning Environment, Forestry and Wildlife.
  10. Botanical Survey of India and Botanical Gardens.
  11. Zoological Survey of India.
  12. National Museum of Natural History.
  13. Biosphere Reserve Programme.
  14. National Forest Policy and Forestry Development in the country, including Social Forestry.
  15. All matters relating to Forest and Forest Administration in the Union territories.
  16. Indian Forest Service.
  17. Wild Life Preservation and protection of wild birds and animals.
  18. Fundamental and applied research and training including higher education in forestry.
  19. Padmaja Naidu Himalayan Zoological Park.
  20. National Assistance to Forestry Development Schemes.
  21. Indian Plywood Industries Research and Training Institute, Bangalore.
  22. Afforestation and Eco-Development which shall include National Afforestation and Eco-Development Board.
  23. Bio-fuel plantations in forest, wastelands and environmental issues concerning bio-fuels.
  24. Desert and Desertification.
  25. Forest Survey of India.
  26. Indian Institute of Bio-diversity, Itanagar.
  27. Central Pollution Control Board.
  28. G.B.Pant Institute of Himalayan Environment & Development.
  29. Wildlife Institute of India and Indian Board for Wildlife.
  30. Indian Institute of Forest Management.
  31. Central Zoo Authority including National Zoo Park.
  32. Indian Council of Forestry Research & Education.
  33. Andaman and Nicobar Islands Forest and Plantation Development Corporation Limited.
  34. Climate change and all other matters related thereto.
  35. The Water (Prevention and Control of Pollution) Act, 1974 (6 of 1974).
  36. The Water (Prevention and Control of Pollution) Cess Act, 1977 (36 of 1977).
  37. The Air (Prevention and Control of Pollution) Act, 1981 (14 of 1981).
  38. The Indian Forest Act, 1927 (16 of 1927).
  39. The Wild Life (Protection) Act, 1972 (53 of 1972)
  40. The Forest (Conservation) Act, 1980 (69 of 1980).
  41. The Environment (Protection) Act, 1986 (29 of 1986).
  42. The Public Liability Insurance Act, 1991 (6 of 1991). NOTE:- The Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change will be responsible for overall policy in relation to forests, except all matters, including legislation, relating to the rights of forest dwelling Schedule Tribes on forest lands.
  43. The National Green Tribunal Act, 2010 (19 of 2010).
