Ministry of Coal

Ministry · Infrastructure & energy · 2000–2001 · its line since 1957 · abolished

On the chartIts section of the RulesAll ministries

Before 2004 the readings of the scanned gazettes have known gaps, so some changes may be missing or read loosely.

Its line: Irrigation & Power 1957–1974 → Energy 1974–1985 → Irrigation and Power 1985 → Energy 1985–1991 → Coal 1991–1999 → Mines and Minerals 1999–2000 → Coal 2000–2001

The units it carries on, through its splits and mergers, back to where it began. Its changes and its story in cells below follow the whole line.

Its line goes on in: Ministry of Coal and Mines 2001–2003, Ministry of Coal 2003–2004, Ministry of Coal and Mines 2004, Ministry of Coal since 2004

Came from: Ministry of Planning and River Valley Schemes, Ministry of Steel and Mines, Ministry of Petroleum, Chemicals and Fertilizers, Ministry of Irrigation, Ministry of Steel, Mines and Coal, Ministry of Steel and Mines, Ministry of Mines and Minerals

Gave subjects to: Ministry of Agriculture and Irrigation, Ministry of Steel, Mines and Coal, Ministry of Petroleum, Ministry of Steel, Mines and Coal, Ministry of Water Resources, Ministry of Power and Non-Conventional Energy Sources, Ministry of Mines

Succeeded by: Ministry of Coal and Mines

Where a merger or a split kept one unit going, it is the one that brought or kept the larger share of subjects.
