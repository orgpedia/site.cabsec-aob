Ministry of Industry

Ministry · Economy & industry · 1963–1999 · its line since 1947 · abolished

Earlier: Ministry of Industry and Company Affairs 1984–1985 · Ministry of Industry 1976–1984 · Ministry of Industry and Civil Supplies 1974–1976 · Ministry of Industrial Development 1971–1974 · Ministry of Industrial Development and Internal Trade 1970–1971 · Ministry of Industrial Development, Internal Trade and Company Affairs 1969–1970 · Ministry of Industrial Development & Company Affairs 1967–1969 · Ministry of Industry 1966–1967 · Ministry of Industry and Supply 1964–1966 · Ministry of Industry 1963–1964

On the chartIts section of the RulesAll ministries

Before 2004 the readings of the scanned gazettes have known gaps, so some changes may be missing or read loosely.

Its line: Industry & Supply 1947–1952 → Commerce & Industry 1952–1963 → Industry 1963–1999

The units it carries on, through its splits and mergers, back to where it began. Its changes and its story in cells below follow the whole line.

Its line goes on in: Ministry of Commerce and Industry since 1999, Ministry of Heavy Industries since 1999, Ministry of Small Scale Industries and Agro and Rural Industries 1999–2001, Ministry of Agro and Rural Industries 2001–2007, Ministry of Small Scale Industries 2001–2007

Came from: Ministry of Commerce, Ministry of Economic and Defence Co-ordination, Ministry of Supply and Technical Development, Ministry of Heavy Industry, Ministry of Commerce, Ministry of Chemicals and Fertilizers, Ministry of Commerce & Industry

Gave subjects to: Ministry of International Trade, Ministry of Supply and Technical Development, Department of Company Affairs, Ministry of Commerce, Ministry of Heavy Industry, Ministry of Civil Supplies and Cooperation

Succeeded by: Ministry of Heavy Industries and Public Enterprises, Ministry of Commerce and Industry, Ministry of Small Scale Industries and Agro and Rural Industries

Where a merger or a split kept one unit going, it is the one that brought or kept the larger share of subjects.
