Ministry of Supply and Textiles

Ministry · Economy & industry · 1985 · its line since 1976 · abolished

On the chartIts section of the RulesAll ministries

Before 2004 the readings of the scanned gazettes have known gaps, so some changes may be missing or read loosely.

Its line: Civil Supplies and Cooperation 1976–1978 → Commerce and Civil Supplies 1978–1980 → Commerce and Supply 1980–1985 → Supply and Textiles 1985

The units it carries on, through its splits and mergers, back to where it began. Its changes and its story in cells below follow the whole line.

Came from: Ministry of Industry, Ministry of Commerce, Ministry of Supply and Rehabilitation, Ministry of Commerce

Gave subjects to: Ministry of Civil Supplies, Ministry of Commerce

Succeeded by: Ministry of Commerce

Where a merger or a split kept one unit going, it is the one that brought or kept the larger share of subjects.
