Ministry of Small Scale Industries

Ministry · Economy & industry · 2001–2007 · its line since 1947 · abolished

On the chartIts section of the RulesAll ministries

Before 2004 the readings of the scanned gazettes have known gaps, so some changes may be missing or read loosely.

Its line: Industry & Supply 1947–1952 → Commerce & Industry 1952–1963 → Industry 1963–1999 → Small Scale Industries and Agro and Rural Industries 1999–2001 → Small Scale Industries 2001–2007

The units it carries on, through its splits and mergers, back to where it began. Its changes and its story in cells below follow the whole line.

Came from: Ministry of Commerce, Ministry of Economic and Defence Co-ordination, Ministry of Supply and Technical Development, Ministry of Heavy Industry, Ministry of Commerce, Ministry of Chemicals and Fertilizers, Ministry of Small Scale Industries and Agro and Rural Industries

Gave subjects to: Ministry of International Trade, Ministry of Supply and Technical Development, Department of Company Affairs, Ministry of Commerce, Ministry of Heavy Industry, Ministry of Civil Supplies and Cooperation, Ministry of Heavy Industries and Public Enterprises, Ministry of Commerce and Industry, Ministry of Agro and Rural Industries

Where a merger or a split kept one unit going, it is the one that brought or kept the larger share of subjects.
