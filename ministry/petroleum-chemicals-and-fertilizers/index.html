Ministry of Petroleum, Chemicals and Fertilizers

Ministry · Infrastructure & energy · 1978–1982 · its line since 1963 · abolished

On the chartIts section of the RulesAll ministries

Before 2004 the readings of the scanned gazettes have known gaps, so some changes may be missing or read loosely.

Its line: Petroleum & Chemicals 1963–1969 → Petroleum and Chemicals and Mines & Metals 1969–1971 → Petroleum and Chemicals 1971–1976 → Chemicals and Fertilisers 1976–1978 → Petroleum, Chemicals and Fertilizers 1978–1982

The units it carries on, through its splits and mergers, back to where it began. Its changes and its story in cells below follow the whole line.

Its line goes on in: Ministry of Chemicals and Fertilizers 1982–1985

Came from: Ministry of Steel, Mines & Metals, Ministry of Petroleum, Ministry of Chemicals and Fertilisers

Gave subjects to: Ministry of Steel and Mines, Ministry of Petroleum

Succeeded by: Ministry of Energy, Ministry of Chemicals and Fertilizers

Where a merger or a split kept one unit going, it is the one that brought or kept the larger share of subjects.
