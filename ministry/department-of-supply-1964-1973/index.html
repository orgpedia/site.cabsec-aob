Department of Supply

Department · Infrastructure & energy · 1964–1973 · its line since 1947 · abolished

Earlier: Ministry of Supply 1969–1971 · Ministry of Works, Housing & Supply 1967–1969 · Ministry of Works, Housing and Urban Development 1966–1967 · Ministry of Works & Housing 1964–1966

On the chartIts section of the RulesAll ministries

Before 2004 the readings of the scanned gazettes have known gaps, so some changes may be missing or read loosely.

Its line: Works, Mines & Power 1947–1952 → Works, Housing and Rehabilitation 1952–1964 → Supply 1964–1973

The units it carries on, through its splits and mergers, back to where it began. Its changes and its story in cells below follow the whole line.

Came from: Ministry of Rehabilitation, Ministry of Supply and Technical Development, Ministry of Works, Housing & Supply

Gave subjects to: Ministry of Production, Ministry of Planning and River Valley Schemes, Ministry of Rehabilitation, Ministry of Health and Family Planning, and Works Housing & Urban Development

Succeeded by: Ministry of Supply and Rehabilitation

Where a merger or a split kept one unit going, it is the one that brought or kept the larger share of subjects.
