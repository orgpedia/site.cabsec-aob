Ministry of Tourism and Civil Aviation

Ministry · Infrastructure & energy · 1967–1985 · its line since 1964 · abolished

Earlier: Ministry of Civil Aviation 1982–1983 · Ministry of Tourism & Civil Aviation 1967–1982

Its line: Civil Aviation 1964–1966 → Transport and Aviation 1966–1967 → Tourism and Civil Aviation 1967–1985

The units it carries on, through its splits and mergers, back to where it began. Its changes and its story in cells below follow the whole line.

Its line goes on in: Ministry of Civil Aviation and Tourism 1986–1989, Ministry of Civil Aviation since 1989, Ministry of Tourism 1989–2000

Came from: Ministry of Transport, Ministry of Tourism, Ministry of Transport and Aviation

Gave subjects to: Ministry of Transport & Shipping, Ministry of Tourism

Succeeded by: Ministry of Transport

Where a merger or a split kept one unit going, it is the one that brought or kept the larger share of subjects.
