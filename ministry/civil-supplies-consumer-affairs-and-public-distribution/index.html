Ministry of Civil Supplies, Consumer Affairs and Public Distribution

Ministry · Economy & industry · 1991–1997 · its line since 1976 · abolished

Earlier: Ministry of Civil Supplies & Public Distribution 1991

Its line: Civil Supplies and Cooperation 1976–1978 → Commerce and Civil Supplies 1978–1980 → Food and Civil Supplies 1980–1991 → Civil Supplies, Consumer Affairs and Public Distribution 1991–1997

The units it carries on, through its splits and mergers, back to where it began. Its changes and its story in cells below follow the whole line.

Its line goes on in: Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food and Public Distribution since 1997

Came from: Ministry of Industry, Ministry of Commerce, Ministry of Civil Supplies

Gave subjects to: Ministry of Commerce, Ministry of Food

Succeeded by: Ministry of Food and Consumer Affairs

Where a merger or a split kept one unit going, it is the one that brought or kept the larger share of subjects.
