Department of Supply

Department · Social & culture · 1973–1985 · its line since 1947 · abolished

Earlier: Ministry of Supply 1982–1983 · Ministry of Supply and Rehabilitation 1973–1982

On the chartIts section of the RulesAll ministries

Before 2004 the readings of the scanned gazettes have known gaps, so some changes may be missing or read loosely.

Its line: Works, Mines & Power 1947–1952 → Works, Housing and Rehabilitation 1952–1964 → Rehabilitation 1964–1966 → Labour and Rehabilitation 1966–1973 → Supply 1973–1985

The units it carries on, through its splits and mergers, back to where it began. Its changes and its story in cells below follow the whole line.

Came from: Ministry of Rehabilitation, Ministry of Labour, Ministry of Works & Housing, Ministry of Labour, Employment and Rehabilitation

Gave subjects to: Ministry of Production, Ministry of Planning and River Valley Schemes, Ministry of Works & Housing, Ministry of Labour

Succeeded by: Ministry of Commerce and Supply

Where a merger or a split kept one unit going, it is the one that brought or kept the larger share of subjects.
