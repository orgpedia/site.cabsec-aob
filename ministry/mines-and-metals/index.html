Its line: Steel and Mines 1963–1966 → Mines and Metals 1966–1967

The units it carries on, through its splits and mergers, back to where it began. Its changes and its story in cells below follow the whole line.

Its line goes on in: Ministry of Steel, Mines & Metals 1967–1969, Ministry of Steel and Mines 1971–1985, Ministry of Steel, Mines and Coal 1985, Ministry of Steel and Mines 1985–1991, Ministry of Mines 1991–1998, Ministry of Steel and Mines 1998–1999, Ministry of Mines and Minerals 1999–2000, Ministry of Mines 2000–2001, Ministry of Mines 2003–2004, Ministry of Mines since 2004

Came from: Ministry of Steel & Heavy Industries, Ministry of Steel, Mines & Heavy Engineering

Gave subjects to: Ministry of Iron and Steel

Succeeded by: Ministry of Steel, Mines & Metals

Where a merger or a split kept one unit going, it is the one that brought or kept the larger share of subjects.
