Ministry of Civil Aviation

Ministry · Infrastructure & energy · 1964–1966 · abolished

On the chartIts section of the RulesAll ministries

Before 2004 the readings of the scanned gazettes have known gaps, so some changes may be missing or read loosely.

Its line goes on in: Ministry of Tourism and Civil Aviation 1967–1985, Ministry of Civil Aviation and Tourism 1986–1989, Ministry of Civil Aviation since 1989

Came from: Ministry of Transport

Succeeded by: Ministry of Transport and Aviation

Where a merger or a split kept one unit going, it is the one that brought or kept the larger share of subjects.
