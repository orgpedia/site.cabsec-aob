Ministry of Civil Supplies and Cooperation

Ministry · Economy & industry · 1976–1978 · abolished

On the chartIts section of the RulesAll ministries

Before 2004 the readings of the scanned gazettes have known gaps, so some changes may be missing or read loosely.

Its line goes on in: Ministry of Commerce and Civil Supplies 1978–1980, Ministry of Food and Civil Supplies 1980–1991, Ministry of Supply and Textiles 1985, Ministry of Civil Supplies, Consumer Affairs and Public Distribution 1991–1997, Ministry of Food 1991–1997, Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food and Public Distribution since 1997

Came from: Ministry of Industry

Succeeded by: Ministry of Commerce, Civil Supplies and Cooperation

Where a merger or a split kept one unit going, it is the one that brought or kept the larger share of subjects.
