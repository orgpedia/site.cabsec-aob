Ministry of Steel & Heavy Industries

Ministry · Economy & industry · 1962–1963 · its line since 1947 · abolished

On the chartIts section of the RulesAll ministries

Before 2004 the readings of the scanned gazettes have known gaps, so some changes may be missing or read loosely.

Its line: Works, Mines & Power 1947–1952 → Steel, Mines & Fuel 1952–1962 → Steel & Heavy Industries 1962–1963

The units it carries on, through its splits and mergers, back to where it began. Its changes and its story in cells below follow the whole line.

Came from: Ministry of Production

Gave subjects to: Ministry of Works, Housing & Supply, Ministry of Planning and River Valley Schemes, Ministry of Mines & Fuel

Succeeded by: Ministry of Steel, Mines & Heavy Engineering

Where a merger or a split kept one unit going, it is the one that brought or kept the larger share of subjects.

Its changes

  1. 13 May 1952
    Jawaharlal Nehru
    SplitWorks, Mines and Power breaks up: Works, Housing and Supply; Production; and river valley schemes go to Planning.
    With: Ministry of Works, Housing & Supply, Ministry of Planning and River Valley Schemes
  2. 16 April 1957
    Jawaharlal Nehru
    RenamedMinistry of Production is renamed Ministry of Steel, Mines & Fuel
  3. 9 April 1962
    Jawaharlal Nehru
    SplitSteel, Mines and Fuel divides into Mines and Fuel, and Steel and Heavy Industries.
    With: Ministry of Mines & Fuel
    Amendment #5 · change 2, 3, 8 · the Rules after it
    The subjects it took (3)
    • Steel plants in the public and private sectors, the rerolling industry and ferro-alloys including all future development.
    • Development of ore mines, coal washeries etc., for steel plants.
    • Production, distribution, prices imports and exports of iron and steel and ferro-alloys.
  4. 21 November 1963
    Jawaharlal Nehru
    AbsorbedMinistry of Steel & Heavy Industries joins Ministry of Steel, Mines & Heavy Engineering (5 subjects)
    With: Ministry of Steel, Mines & Heavy Engineering
    Amendment #22 · change 3, 4, 5, 16, 21 · the Rules before it
    The subjects that left (5)
    • Steel plants in the public and private sectors, the rerolling industry and ferro-alloys including all future development.
    • Development of ore mines, coal washeries etc., for steel plants.
    • Production, distribution, prices, imports and exports or iron and steel and ferro-alloys.
    • Setting up of synthetic oil plant based on coal and low temperature carbonisation plants for the production of smokeless domestic coal.
    • Exploration of Lignite deposits in India.

Its subjects

Its subjects when it ended (1963) · 24
  1. Steel plants in the public and private sectors, the rerolling industry and ferro-alloys including all future development.
  2. Development of ore mines, coal washeries etc., for steel plants.
  3. Production, distribution, prices imports and exports of iron and steel and ferro-alloys.
  4. Administration of the following offices: (a) Office of the Coal Controller. (b) Office of the Coal Board. (c) Office of the Coal Grading Board. (d) Geological Survey of India. (e) Indian Bureau of Mines. (f) Oil & Natural Gas Commission.
  5. Production, supply, distribution and prices of coal and coke.
  6. National Coal Development Corporation Ltd., Ranchi.
  7. Neyveli Lignite Corporation Ltd., Madras, and matters connected with the exploration of lignite deposits in other parts of India.
  8. The Indian Mining and Construction Co. Ltd., Calcutta.
  9. Setting up of synthetic oil plant and low temperature carbonisation plants for the production of smokeless domestic coal.
  10. Coal Council of India.
  11. Administration of the Coal Mines (Conservation and Safety) Act, 1952.
  12. Administration of the Coal Grading Board Act, 1955.
  13. Administration of Coal Bearing Areas (Acquisition & Development) Act, 1957.
  14. Regulation of mines and mineral development under the Mines & Minerals (Regulation and Development) Act, 1957 and other Union laws including questions concerning various States and incidental business in respect of these.
  15. Mineral Advisory Board.
  16. Orissa Mining Corporation Ltd., Bhubneswar.
  17. Production, supply, distribution and prices of petroleum and petroleum products.
  18. Exploration for and exploitation of oil resources in India including the setting up of participatory projects e.g., Indo-Stanvac Petroleum Project, Oil India Ltd., etc.
  19. Setting up of oil refineries in India.
  20. Production of refinery products by: (i) Standard Vacuum Refinery Company, Bombay. (ii) Burmah-Shell Refinery, Bombay. (iii) Caltex Refinery Visakhapatnam; and (iv) Assam Oil Company Ltd., Digboi.
  21. Setting up of lubricating oil plants.
  22. Setting up of a distribution organisation for the sale of petroleum and petroleum products.
  23. Utilisation and establishment of industries based on natural gas and refinery by-products e.g., petro-chemicals, fertilizers, power-generation stations etc.
  24. Petroleum Consumers Advisory Council.
