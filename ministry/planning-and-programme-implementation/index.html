Ministry of Planning and Programme Implementation

Ministry · Centre of government · 1952–1999 · its line since 1947 · abolished

Earlier: Ministry of Planning 1971–1991 · Planning Commission 1961–1971 · Ministry of Planning and River Valley Schemes 1952–1961

On the chartIts section of the RulesAll ministries

Before 2004 the readings of the scanned gazettes have known gaps, so some changes may be missing or read loosely.

Its line: Works, Mines & Power 1947–1952 → Planning and Programme Implementation 1952–1999

The units it carries on, through its splits and mergers, back to where it began. Its changes and its story in cells below follow the whole line.

Its line goes on in: Ministry of Planning since 1999, Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation since 1999

Came from: Department of Science and Technology

Gave subjects to: Ministry of Works, Housing & Supply, Ministry of Production, Ministry of Irrigation & Power, Planning Commission, Department of Science and Technology

Succeeded by: Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation, Ministry of Planning
