Ministry of Health and Family Welfare

Ministry · Social & culture · since 1971 · its line since 1947

Formerly: Ministry of Health and Family Planning 1971–1977

Its line: Health and Family Planning, and Works Housing & Urban Development 1947–1971 → Health and Family Welfare since 1971

The units it carries on, through its splits and mergers, back to where it began. Its changes and its story in cells below follow the whole line.

Came from: Ministry of Works, Housing & Supply, Ministry of Health

Gave subjects to: Ministry of Works and Housing, Ministry of Ayurveda, Yoga and Naturopathy, Unani, Siddha and Homoeopathy (AYUSH)

Where a merger or a split kept one unit going, it is the one that brought or kept the larger share of subjects.
