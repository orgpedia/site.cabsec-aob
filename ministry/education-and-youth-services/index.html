Ministry of Education and Youth Services

Ministry · Social & culture · 1947–1971 · abolished

Earlier: Ministry of Education 1961–1969 · Ministry of Education and Scientific Research 1957–1961 · Ministry of Education 1947–1957

On the chartIts section of the RulesAll ministries

Before 2004 the readings of the scanned gazettes have known gaps, so some changes may be missing or read loosely.

Its line goes on in: Ministry of Education and Social Welfare 1971–1979, Department of Culture 1971–1979, Department of Science and Technology 1971–1975, Ministry of Education and Culture 1979–1985, Ministry of Social and Women's Welfare 1979–1985, Ministry of Education 1985, Ministry of Cultural Affairs 1985, Ministry of Education since 1985, Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment since 1985

Came from: Ministry of Scientific Research, Ministry of Scientific Research & Cultural Affairs

Gave subjects to: Ministry of Scientific Research & Cultural Affairs, Department of Social Security

Succeeded by: Ministry of Education and Social Welfare, Department of Culture, Department of Science and Technology
