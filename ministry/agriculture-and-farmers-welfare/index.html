Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare

Ministry · Land, water & rural · since 1947 · its line since 1957

Formerly: Ministry of Agriculture 1985–2015 · Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Development 1985 · Ministry of Agriculture 1980–1985 · Ministry of Agriculture and Irrigation 1974–1980 · Ministry of Agriculture 1971–1974 · Ministry of Food, Agriculture, Community Development and Cooperation 1966–1971 · Ministry of Food & Agriculture 1961–1966

On the chartIts section of the RulesAll ministries

Before 2004 the readings of the scanned gazettes have known gaps, so some changes may be missing or read loosely.

Its line: Irrigation & Power 1957–1974 → Agriculture since 1947

The units it carries on, through its splits and mergers, back to where it began. Its changes and its story in cells below follow the whole line.

Came from: Ministry of Planning and River Valley Schemes, Ministry of Community Development, Ministry of Rural Reconstruction, Ministry of Chemicals and Fertilizers, Ministry of Food Processing Industries

Gave subjects to: Ministry of Energy, Ministry of Irrigation, Ministry of Rural Development, Ministry of Food Processing Industries, Ministry of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry and Dairying, Ministry of Cooperation
