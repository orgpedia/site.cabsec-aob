Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment

Ministry · Social & culture · since 1985 · its line since 1947

Formerly: Ministry of Welfare 1985–1998

On the chartIts section of the RulesAll ministries

Before 2004 the readings of the scanned gazettes have known gaps, so some changes may be missing or read loosely.

Its line: Education and Youth Services 1947–1971 → Education and Social Welfare 1971–1979 → Social and Women's Welfare 1979–1985 → Social Justice since 1985

The units it carries on, through its splits and mergers, back to where it began. Its changes and its story in cells below follow the whole line.

Came from: Ministry of Scientific Research, Ministry of Scientific Research & Cultural Affairs, Department of Social Security, Ministry of Social Welfare

Gave subjects to: Ministry of Scientific Research & Cultural Affairs, Department of Social Security, Department of Culture, Department of Science and Technology, Ministry of Education and Culture, Ministry of Human Resource Development, Ministry of Minority Affairs

Where a merger or a split kept one unit going, it is the one that brought or kept the larger share of subjects.
