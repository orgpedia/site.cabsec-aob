Ministry of Steel and Mines

Ministry · Infrastructure & energy · 1985–1991 · its line since 1963 · abolished

On the chartIts section of the RulesAll ministries

Before 2004 the readings of the scanned gazettes have known gaps, so some changes may be missing or read loosely.

Its line: Steel and Mines 1963–1966 → Mines and Metals 1966–1967 → Steel, Mines & Metals 1967–1969 → Petroleum and Chemicals and Mines & Metals 1969–1971 → Steel and Mines 1971–1985 → Steel, Mines and Coal 1985 → Steel and Mines 1985–1991

The units it carries on, through its splits and mergers, back to where it began. Its changes and its story in cells below follow the whole line.

Its line goes on in: Ministry of Mines 1991–1998, Ministry of Steel 1991–1998, Ministry of Steel and Mines 1998–1999, Ministry of Mines and Minerals 1999–2000, Ministry of Steel since 1999, Ministry of Mines 2000–2001, Ministry of Mines 2003–2004, Ministry of Mines since 2004

Came from: Ministry of Steel & Heavy Industries, Ministry of Iron and Steel, Ministry of Petroleum & Chemicals, Ministry of Steel and Heavy Engineering, Ministry of Energy, Ministry of Steel, Mines and Coal

Gave subjects to: Ministry of Iron and Steel, Ministry of Steel and Heavy Engineering, Ministry of Petroleum and Chemicals, Ministry of Energy, Ministry of Energy

Succeeded by: Ministry of Steel, Ministry of Mines

Where a merger or a split kept one unit going, it is the one that brought or kept the larger share of subjects.
