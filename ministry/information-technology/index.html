Ministry of Information Technology

Ministry · Infrastructure & energy · 1971–2001 · abolished

Earlier: Department of Electronics 1971–1999

On the chartIts section of the RulesAll ministries

Before 2004 the readings of the scanned gazettes have known gaps, so some changes may be missing or read loosely.

Its line goes on in: Ministry of Communications and Information Technology 2001–2016, Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology since 2016

Succeeded by: Ministry of Communications and Information Technology
