Ministry of Foreign Trade

Ministry · Economy & industry · 1963–1973 · its line since 1947 · abolished

Earlier: Ministry of Foreign Trade and Supply 1969 · Ministry of Commerce 1964–1969 · Ministry of International Trade 1963–1964

On the chartIts section of the RulesAll ministries

Before 2004 the readings of the scanned gazettes have known gaps, so some changes may be missing or read loosely.

Its line: Industry & Supply 1947–1952 → Commerce & Industry 1952–1963 → Foreign Trade 1963–1973

The units it carries on, through its splits and mergers, back to where it began. Its changes and its story in cells below follow the whole line.

Came from: Ministry of Commerce, Ministry of Commerce & Industry

Gave subjects to: Ministry of Industry, Ministry of Commerce

Where a merger or a split kept one unit going, it is the one that brought or kept the larger share of subjects.
