Ministry · Social & culture · 1966–1973 · its line since 1947 · abolished

Its line: Labour & Employment 1947–1966 → Labour and Rehabilitation 1966–1973

The units it carries on, through its splits and mergers, back to where it began. Its changes and its story in cells below follow the whole line.

Came from: Ministry of Rehabilitation, Ministry of Labour

Succeeded by: Ministry of Labour, Ministry of Supply and Rehabilitation

Where a merger or a split kept one unit going, it is the one that brought or kept the larger share of subjects.
