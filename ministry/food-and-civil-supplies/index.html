Ministry of Food and Civil Supplies

Ministry · Economy & industry · 1980–1991 · its line since 1976 · abolished

Earlier: Ministry of Civil Supplies 1980–1983

On the chartIts section of the RulesAll ministries

Before 2004 the readings of the scanned gazettes have known gaps, so some changes may be missing or read loosely.

Its line: Civil Supplies and Cooperation 1976–1978 → Commerce and Civil Supplies 1978–1980 → Food and Civil Supplies 1980–1991

The units it carries on, through its splits and mergers, back to where it began. Its changes and its story in cells below follow the whole line.

Its line goes on in: Ministry of Civil Supplies, Consumer Affairs and Public Distribution 1991–1997, Ministry of Food 1991–1997, Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food and Public Distribution since 1997

Came from: Ministry of Industry, Ministry of Commerce, Ministry of Commerce, Civil Supplies and Cooperation

Gave subjects to: Ministry of Commerce

Succeeded by: Ministry of Civil Supplies & Public Distribution, Ministry of Food

Where a merger or a split kept one unit going, it is the one that brought or kept the larger share of subjects.
